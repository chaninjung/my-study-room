# My Study Room — ELTE AI & Computer Science Study Hub

**My Study Room** is a study site for **ELTE (Eötvös Loránd University)** students — especially those in **AI and Computer Science** — to prepare for university courses.

It started as a personal study room for the **Deep Network Development (DND)** course and has been expanded so ELTE students can log in and study together.

## Why this site exists

From **2026 Autumn**, ELTE Computer Science course content is available here so students can study for **preliminary exams** through practice questions.

- **Who can join:** Only users with an **`@inf.elte.hu`** email address can sign up. Anyone can find this site through Google (search: **inf.elte.hu**), but only ELTE inf accounts can create an account and log in.
- **Current courses:** **Deep Network Development (DND)** and **Introduction to Data Science**.
- **Purpose:** Practice with **mock exam** questions and take **exam practice** sessions to prepare for the real ELTE exam. Introduction to Data Science currently includes the course calendar and a Markdown question bank; interactive question sets will be enabled as they are converted to the app format.
